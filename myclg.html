Introduction

Chitkara University is a private university located in Patiala, Punjab, established in 2010. It is recognised by UGC and has been accredited with an A+ Grade by NAAC. It holds the 52nd rank in the Nursing category by Collegedunia 2025 ranking. It has also been ranked 78th in the MBA category by the NIRF ranking 2025. MBA, BE, and B.Sc (Nursing) are the most preferred programs at Chitkara University, while the MBA in Human Resource Management and BE in CSE are the most preferred choices by students. My college is a place of learning and growth, where students come together to pursue their academic and personal goals. It provides a supportive environment that encourages intellectual curiosity, creativity, and critical thinking.

    • FACILITIES

    Chitkara University provides state-of-the-art facilities to support the academic and personal development of its students. Some of the key facilities include:-

    • Modern classrooms with smart boards
    • Well-equipped laboratories for various disciplines
    • Library with a vast collection of books and journals
    • Hostel facilities for both male and female students
    • Medical center for student health services
    • Sports facilities including a gym, tennis courts, and a football field

    Library

    The library at Chitkara University is a hub of knowledge and learning, offering a wide range of resources to support the academic needs of students and faculty. It features an extensive collection of books, journals, e-resources, and research papers across various disciplines. The library is equipped with modern facilities, including digital access to online databases, study areas, and computer terminals for research purposes. It serves as a conducive environment for reading, studying, and conducting research.

    Laboratories

    Chitkara University boasts well-equipped laboratories that cater to the practical learning needs of students across different fields of study. These labs are designed to provide hands-on experience and facilitate experimentation, research, and innovation. Each department has its own specialized laboratories, such as computer labs for IT and CSE students, engineering labs for mechanical and civil engineering students, and science labs for nursing and other health-related programs. The laboratories are maintained with the latest equipment and technology to ensure that students gain relevant skills and knowledge in their respective areas of study.

    Cafeteria

    The cafeteria at Chitkara University offers a variety of nutritious and delicious food options to cater to the diverse tastes and dietary preferences of students, faculty, and staff. It provides a comfortable and welcoming environment for dining, socializing, and relaxation. The cafeteria serves a range of meals, snacks, beverages, and desserts, ensuring that there are options available for everyone. It also emphasizes hygiene and quality in food preparation and service, making it a popular spot for students to gather during breaks between classes or after academic activities.
