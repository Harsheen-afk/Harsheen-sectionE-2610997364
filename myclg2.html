Introduction

Chitkara University is a private university located in Punjab. The university campus is situated on the Chandigarh-Patiala National Highway.

Chitkara University provides a supportive environment where students come together to pursue their academic and personal goals.

The university focuses on learning, creativity, innovation and practical education.

Students get opportunities to develop their skills through academics, clubs, sports, events and extracurricular activities.
